## Title

PINK TROMBONE on the M-VAVE FM-1: a singing voice synth (custom firmware)

## Description

A voice synthesizer for the M-VAVE FM-1: Neil Thapen's Pink Trombone, a glottis and a modelled throat, mouth, tongue, lips
and nose, as a firmware for the pocket FM synth. Nothing is sampled: the vowels come from the shape of the tract.

Recorded in the fm1-emu emulator (the firmware's real screen and audio, rendered offline). Not yet tried on a real FM-1.

0:00 Intro
0:05 Vowels (PRESETS)
0:17 Play it: keys, KNOB1-3 move the tongue and lips
0:26 Consonants: hold a button to close the mouth (P T K, SH S F, M)
0:37 FX: chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb, in stereo
0:47 End

Controls: keys/MIDI pitch; KNOB1 tongue position, KNOB2 tongue height, KNOB3 lips, KNOB4 tenseness; PRESETS a vowel;
SELECT vibrato; ALGORITHM nose; hold SEQ/SEL/ENV (S, SH, F), LFO/EDIT/GLO (T, K, P), ARP (M/N); FX effects page; PLAY drone.

Credits: Pink Trombone by Neil Thapen (MIT). Built on fm1-dock by Joakim Eriksson, on the platform of Felucca by Leo Kuroshita
(Hugelton Instruments). FM-1 emulator: fm1-emu by Joakim Eriksson. Not affiliated with M-VAVE. Installing firmware is at your own risk.
